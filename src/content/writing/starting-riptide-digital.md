I've started something on the side. It's called
[Riptide Digital](https://riptidedigital.com.au), and it's where I'm going to build and
release my own apps from here in Newcastle.

It's a side hustle for now. I've still got a day job, so this happens at night and on
weekends. But it's real, it has a website, and the first app is well underway.

## How it started

I have psoriatic arthritis. If you've got it, or anything like it, you'll know the
drill. You see your rheumatologist every few months, they ask how it's been, and you try
to remember. Was last month worse than the one before? When did that flare start? Which
joints? I never had good answers.

So I built myself an app to track it. It was just for me, and it was only ever meant to
cover PsA. The more I used it, the more I realised plenty of other people are in the
same spot with all sorts of conditions. That little app turned into the idea for
Riptide Digital.

## Two sides

Riptide Digital is going to make two kinds of things: utility apps and games.

### Utility apps

These are the useful ones. The first is **PainTrail**, a pain diary for anyone living
with ongoing pain. It grew straight out of my PsA app. You do a quick check-in each day:
how bad it is, where it hurts, how you slept. Over time it builds a picture you can
actually show your doctor. Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, and you
don't need the internet. It's coming to Android first, then iPhone. There's more on the
[PainTrail project page](/building/paintrail/).

After that comes **MindTrail**, a partner app for mental health. This one matters a lot
to me. My family is heavily affected by neurodivergence and mental health concerns, and
I've seen how hard it can be to keep track of what's going on, let alone make things
better. I'd love to make that easier. It's a little way off yet, but it's coming.

Both apps will work the same way: private by default, and a free version that's
actually useful on its own.

### Games

The other side is games. I write fiction, and I've always loved games and how their
worlds work, so this was never going to be all serious apps. I'm starting with small,
fun mobile games. The first one is in early planning, and I'll share more once there's
something worth showing.

## What's next

I'll write about both sides here as I go, including the bits that go wrong. I'm learning
a lot of this as I go: store accounts, privacy policies, business names, the lot. The
code is honestly the easy part some days.

If you want to follow along, [riptidedigital.com.au](https://riptidedigital.com.au) is
where the apps and games will live, and its [About page](https://riptidedigital.com.au/about/)
has a bit more about the studio. I'll keep writing about building them here.
