## Overview

Riptide Digital is my independent app and game studio, based in Newcastle, NSW. It is
where I build and publish apps under one name. It has two sides: utility apps, starting
with PainTrail, a pain diary, and small mobile games. I wrote about why I started it in
[Starting Riptide Digital](/writing/starting-riptide-digital/).

## Context

I have spent years improving systems and reporting inside other organisations. Riptide
Digital is the place where I build my own products from start to finish.

## Problem

Plenty of everyday apps ask for an account, show ads, or collect more data than they
need. I wanted a home for apps that do one job well and respect the person using them.

## Approach

- Privacy first: the core of each app works offline, with no account and no tracking.
- A useful core stays free. Paid extras are optional.
- Small enough to finish: each app does one thing properly.

## Tools

Flutter for the apps, so one codebase covers Android and iPhone. Astro for the website.

## Status

Ongoing. The website is live and still being built out, and PainTrail is in
development.

## What I learned

Running even a very small studio means the building is only part of the job. Store
rules, privacy policies and support all need the same care as the code.
