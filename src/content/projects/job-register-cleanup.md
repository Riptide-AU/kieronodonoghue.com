## Overview

A data cleansing project for a construction business that had no single record of its
jobs. I collated six years of job records into one register that could be filtered and
reported on.

## Context

Quoted prices, costs, clients, contacts and start dates were spread across separate
files covering six years. Answering a simple question about past work meant opening
several of them.

## Problem

The same client was spelt several ways, phone numbers were in mixed formats, and
contacts were duplicated with small differences. Nothing could be counted or compared
reliably.

## Approach

- Brought every job into one Excel register, keeping the original files untouched.
- Standardised text and phone number formats.
- Matched each record to a clean list of clients and contacts, then removed duplicates.
- Added dropdown lists so new entries could only be chosen from the approved clients
  and contacts, with a controlled way to add new ones.

## Tools

Excel, including lookups, data validation and Power Query.

## Status

Completed. The business had one consistent register of jobs, prices, costs and dates.
I have kept the details general here.

## What I learned

Cleaning data once is only half the job. Without a control at the point of entry, the
same mess comes back within months.
