## Overview

A forecasting project for an importer and wholesaler. I moved stock ordering onto a
rolling forecast that was updated every month.

## Context

The business ordered imported stock with long lead times. To avoid running out, it
tended to order more than it needed.

## Problem

Too much money was sitting in stock, and ordering decisions were based on a view of
sales that was often months old.

## Approach

- Built a forecast from recent sales, stock levels and supplier lead times.
- Updated it monthly, so each order reflected the latest picture.
- Used it to guide how much to order and when.

## Tools

Excel, working from the reports in the ordering and inventory system.

## Status

Completed. Average stock holding came down without the business running short. I have
kept the figures general here.

## What I learned

A simple forecast that is updated every month is more useful than a detailed one that
is updated once a year.
